ABOUT HRKAFE

A different kind of recruitment conversation.

HRKAFE brings together the professionalism of talent acquisition with the warmth of a good conversation.

We connect businesses with suitable talent and help professionals discover career opportunities that make sense for their experience, aspirations and next chapter.

The idea is simple: recruitment should feel less transactional and more human—clear conversations, thoughtful matching and relationships that can grow beyond a single placement.

01

Our story

HRKAFE is being built as a people-first recruitment brand where careers, companies and conversations come together.

02

Our philosophy

Look beyond a CV. Understand the person, the role, the context and the possibility of a lasting fit.

03

Our approach

Listen carefully. Search thoughtfully. Communicate clearly. Keep both candidates and employers informed.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Keep this section updated as candidates and employers ask recurring questions.

How can I apply for a job?

Open a current job opportunity and select “Apply Now”, or submit your CV through the HRKAFE candidate form.

Can I submit my CV without applying for a specific job?

Yes. Use the Submit Your CV option and HRKAFE can consider your profile for relevant future opportunities.

How can an employer start a hiring requirement?

Use the employer contact section or submit your hiring requirement through the form linked on the website.

Does HRKAFE work with different industries?

Yes. HRKAFE can support multiple industries and the active focus areas can be updated as the consultancy grows.
